For almost a decade, the National Transition Event (NTE) at Silverstone has helped change life outcomes for thousands of members of the Armed Forces community and their families.

We are delighted to return to Silverstone for NTE27 on Monday 1st March 2027, once again connecting businesses, government departments and service charities along with the Armed Forces community for the largest event of its kind in the UK. The event brings together the community all under one roof in an expo-style format, alongside the much-loved Troops’ Track Day on the GP Circuit. And we want you to play your part too!

Exhibiting alongside organisations whose sponsorship and financial support have enabled a number of free charity spaces, it is an opportunity to widen your engagement and network within industry on an equal footing, saving months of work, calls and coffee meetings!
