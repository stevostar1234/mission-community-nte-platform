## Follow-up: rolling deadlines and payable invoices

These are options raised on 14 September, not approved changes. Payment Links only and manual confirmation remain the selected workflow. Current `NTEStripeClient` applies no date-based expiry and explicitly disables invoice creation.

A reusable deadline policy can calculate and save a date for each payment request when staff first release it. Three calendar months and 90 days are different choices. Reopening or resending the same request should retain its original deadline unless an authorised person explicitly extends it. If needed, cap the rolling deadline at a cutoff belonging to that booking's event year; do not reuse a global 2027 date for future events. Decide what to do with new requests already beyond that event's cutoff.

Distinguish a payment due date from a hard stop on payment. A Salesforce scheduled Flow can use the saved date and the Stripe app's update action to deactivate a Payment Link. This requires implementation, failure monitoring and testing of deadline changes, already-open checkout sessions and payments in progress. A missed due date must not silently cancel an allocation or trigger a refund. [Salesforce scheduled paths](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=platform.flow_concepts_trigger_scheduled_path.htm&language=en_US), [Stripe deactivation](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment-link/update), [official app actions](https://github.com/stripe/salesforce-connector-examples/blob/main/context/invocable-actions-reference.md#paymentlinks).

An invoice issued before payment can preserve the email-button journey: staff release the request, the integration creates the correct customer's draft invoice and its booking-specific items, finalizes it, saves the invoice identity and hosted URL against the new Opportunity, then includes that URL in the NTE email. The hosted page displays invoice details, takes payment and provides PDF downloads. Manual booking confirmation can remain. The app documents invoice creation, item and finalization actions, but the complete NTE Flow and its failure/retry handling still need implementation and qualification. The current Payment Link URL/object validation and VAT representation also need adapting. [Hosted invoice page](https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page), [invoice API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/create), [app invoice actions](https://github.com/stripe/salesforce-connector-examples/blob/main/context/invocable-actions-reference.md#invoices).

Enabling invoice creation on a Payment Link instead generates a **post-purchase invoice**; it does not issue the payable invoice before checkout. [Payment Link invoice creation](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment-link/create#payment_link_create-invoice_creation). Hosted invoice URLs have their own expiry: 30 days after the due date, or after finalization if there is no due date, with a maximum 120-day window. Retrieve a fresh URL when needed for resends. Do not assume Stripe's email recovery applies when only NTE sent the original email. Invoice URL expiry, payment due date and cancellation of the booking are separate concepts. [Invoice URL lifecycle](https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page#invoice-urls).
