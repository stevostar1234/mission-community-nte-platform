## Acceptance details that matter

Contact: a Primary Contact different from the event-day contact is shown and receives post-conversion messages. Missing primary/email remains visible and unsent. No silent fallback or extra recipients. Pre-conversion messages continue to use their Lead.

Logistics: a valid replacement updates/removes the correct items and haulier details, while preserving contacts, billing and finance. An invalid replacement makes no changes.

Top-up: No or invalid attempts do not consume the allowance. First accepted positive quantity locks it. Same quantity with corrected names preserves price and invoice/payment history. Later increases are rejected. Separate transactions and multiple submissions in one bulk operation both respect the first accepted state. The existing in-memory processing must be adjusted as well as the row-lock check. A first top-up after pack distribution may reopen finance without erasing Pack sent.

Email: success updates only the intended booking; missing configuration, changed recipients, failures and simultaneous attempts do not send twice or falsely mark success. Retry the saved request. Update old tests that intentionally assert now-superseded behaviours.

Stripe: a GBP payable total is converted to pence once using the agreed tax treatment. Creation/retrieval confirms the correct fixed price, currency, charge identity, active status and environment under the expected account. A timeout retries using the same durable request and idempotency key. A change in pricing before dispatch leaves the old request ineligible. API/send failures remain visible, and no old conversion path emits a parallel email. Use test credentials and synthetic recipients for integration qualification; independently verify the live account and bank configuration before any separately authorised release.
