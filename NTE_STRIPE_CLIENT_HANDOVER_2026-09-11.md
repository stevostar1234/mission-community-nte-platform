## How a booking travels through the system

| Step | What happens | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Application | The applicant submits an exhibitor or partner/sponsor application. Salesforce receives a Lead without a Stripe request or applicant application-received email. The latest approved form refinement collects invoice Yes/No separately from the payment method. | Applicant and existing Web-to-Lead processing |
| 2. Review and conversion | Every application creates a new Opportunity. Existing Accounts and Contacts may be reused; the new name ends with the saved event code. Conversion copies the application and VAT pricing but sends no email, creates no payment link and confirms no space. | NTE staff |
| 3. Requirements | Staff can record each applicable procurement requirement independently. For Stripe they choose **Requirements provided, send Stripe link**; bank transfer uses **Requirements provided, await payment**. The checkboxes do not gate these actions and sending never ticks them. | NTE staff |
| 4. Save and verify the Stripe charge | Only the explicit Stripe action queues this work. Salesforce checks the saved pricing and Primary Contact, saves the exact charge and creates or reuses its fixed Price and Payment Link. It verifies account, mode, amount, currency, identity and restrictions before sending. | Salesforce calling Stripe |
| 5. Send the payment request | Salesforce rechecks the booking and sends the itemised provisional email and preparation links to its Primary Contact. An accepted Stripe or bank-transfer request moves the booking to **Payment required** once no other charge awaits release. A £0 booking instead needs **Confirm Free space** in Requirements, with no fictitious payment. | Salesforce after the staff action |
| 6. Pay and verify | The applicant pays through Stripe or the agreed bank-transfer route. Staff match the successful receipt to the exact charge, reference, gross amount and currency. They check relevant refund/dispute status; opening checkout is insufficient. | Applicant and NTE finance/operations |
| 7. Confirm the space | In **Payment required**, staff use **Booking payment received**. Salesforce records the receipt and requests one booking-confirmation email. Notification failure does not undo the payment. | NTE staff and Salesforce |
| 8. Later staff top-up | The one permitted positive additional-staff purchase has its own charge and Stripe link, including for a bank-transfer base booking. A paid booking returns to **Requirements**; staff choose **Send Stripe link**, then record **Staff top-up payment received** in Payment required. Original payment and checklist history remain intact. | Applicant, NTE staff and Salesforce |
| 9. Complete preparation | Completed requires valid pricing, original paid/free confirmation, all actual charges paid, and the applicable staff, vehicle and logo preparation. Staff send customised joining instructions themselves and record **Final joining instructions sent** from Completed. The marker does not gate entry to Completed. | NTE staff |

There is no automatic Stripe-to-Salesforce payment notification receiver in this release. A successful Stripe payment therefore does not, by itself, mark the Salesforce booking paid. The manual action does not independently ask Stripe whether payment succeeded: the staff verification is essential.

The booking confirmation does not complete staff, logo or vehicle information. Automated final-pack distribution has been retired from the workflow; historic send evidence remains. Requirements takes precedence whenever another charge still awaits its request, keeping the three finance refiners mutually exclusive.

## Money, documents and recipients

Salesforce's saved booking pricing is the source of the amount. Applicable complimentary entitlements and discounts feed that saved pricing before Stripe receives a charge. A completely free booking does not need a Stripe request; a complimentary space can still have chargeable extras.

The client confirmed **20% VAT added to net NTE prices** on 13 September. The released forms, Salesforce totals, reports, emails and Stripe use the same saved net-plus-VAT calculation after discounts. A £100 net booking has £20 VAT and £120 payable. Stripe receives 12,000 pence as one fixed GBP Price. The reservation email and checkout display the aggregate net/VAT/gross breakdown; package and item prices remain net. Payment Link and PaymentIntent metadata retain the net amount, VAT amount and saved rate. Reconcile collected money with the saved gross payable amount.

**Stripe automatic tax is disabled.** Salesforce has already calculated the VAT, and the fixed Price includes it. Enabling automatic tax as well would change this contract. The checkout disclosure and metadata do not create Stripe Tax accounting entries or formal VAT invoices. A future approved tax-policy change must update the central pricing policy and all affected channels together; existing issued requests retain their saved amounts and must be reconciled before any replacement.

**Stripe invoice creation is disabled.** Sending a payment email records the accepted request, not quotation/invoice checklist completion. Staff record those requirements separately, and their ticks never gate sending or completion. The latest approved applicant invoice choice is independent of payment method; Stripe must not imply No and bank transfer must not imply Yes. That choice is being implemented and qualified. A £0 booking hides inapplicable procurement/finance details and still needs explicit free-space confirmation. The email does not create a numbered formal invoice or prove receipt of a purchase order; the client's separate finance-document process still needs to be agreed.

Salesforce booking emails use the booking's **Primary Contact**, with no automatic finance-contact copy. The payer can supply another email address in Stripe. A Stripe payment receipt and a Salesforce space-confirmation email serve different purposes. Receipt settings belong to the client's Stripe account; current Stripe guidance uses manual receipt sending for test payments. [Stripe receipts](https://docs.stripe.com/receipts).

The requested charge currency is GBP. Payment Links can offer local-currency payment through Adaptive Pricing, so GBP-only customer presentation must not be promised without checking the client's requirements and checkout behavior. A strict GBP-only requirement could affect the selected integration approach. The present URL validator also accepts only `buy.stripe.com`, so a client-specific Stripe checkout domain would require an explicit validator change. [Payment Link customisation](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links/customize).

Money belongs to the Stripe merchant account identified by its credential. The client's payout bank account and payout schedule are configured inside Stripe; the integration does not pass a bank destination with each booking. A successful customer payment and a payout reaching the bank are separate events. Finance must reconcile fees and payouts separately from the customer's gross booking payment. [Stripe payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts).

The integration fixes quantity at one, requests card payments, disables promotion codes and adjustable quantities, and sets a limit of one completed checkout per link. Creation with an unknown outcome older than 23 hours is held for reconciliation, because Stripe's idempotency retention must not be treated as permanent. These controls reduce duplicate and stale requests; they are not a substitute for reconciliation. [Stripe idempotent requests](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests).

Salesforce metadata deployment does not carry the stored secret into the destination org: the destination principal must be populated securely. ## Close Date, revised bookings and cancellations

**Close Date is an expected date, not a timer.** At the 11 September inspection, native converted Opportunities defaulted to 30 September 2026, the end of that fiscal quarter. Passing that date does not set Closed Won or Closed Lost. No active NTE flow, Opportunity workflow rule, Apex trigger or scheduled closure job was found doing that in the inspected scope. A new test booking with `CloseDate=2026-06-11` stayed in Qualification and completed payment on 11 September. [Salesforce Opportunity fields](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.opp_fields.htm&language=en_US).

Closed Won remains included in active NTE views and eligible workflows, subject to the other booking checks. Closed Lost is excluded from active booking views and blocks new payment-request emails. Neither stage change issues a Stripe refund or closes an existing Stripe link. Stripe payment success, refunds and disputes do not update Salesforce automatically under the chosen manual model. Payment, stage and preparation are separate facts. The current finance reports exclude Closed Lost bookings and do not provide a separate refund ledger; retain the refund reference on the booking and reconcile refund amounts in Stripe.

For an agreed booking change, manual bank transfer is a reasonable fallback. Keep the revised total, amount already received, balance and payment reference clear. **If a saved Stripe request exists and the booking's net amount changes, the current automatic confirmation email refuses to render because the saved and current totals differ.** Recording payment is still possible; an agreed manual confirmation or a separately approved reconciliation change is needed. Do not rewrite the old saved payment request solely to suppress this check.

For a cancellation, refund through Stripe as agreed with the client and mark the booking Closed Lost, preserving the original payment and refund history. Wait for the refund outcome before describing it as completed. Partial refunds do not necessarily mean the booking is cancelled. Leaving an unused old link active allows an accidental late payment, so the recommendation is to deactivate superseded or cancelled unpaid links manually. This does not add an automatic cancellation feature.

A Stripe Price's amount cannot be edited in place. The inspected active NTE link also has **More options → Edit** disabled in Stripe's Dashboard. Changing a price requires a new Price; existing booking requests and emailed links do not track later Salesforce totals. Stripe allows editing some other link settings; this does not make these issued NTE amounts adjustable. Do not treat a Dashboard edit as an NTE reconciliation mechanism. Prefer a clearly identified replacement request or the agreed bank-transfer route, with the old link closed and original history retained. [Stripe Prices](https://docs.stripe.com/products-prices/manage-prices).

**Do not make a nominal live charge solely as a test.** Stripe's current testing guidance prohibits testing live mode with real payment details. Use its sandbox cards for simulations, read-only live account/credential checks and checkout inspection for launch readiness, then reconcile the first genuine authorised booking. [Stripe testing guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/testing).
