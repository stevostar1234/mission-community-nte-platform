## What changed

The original allowlist limited early integration tests to one owner's email and the special `NTE-STRIPE-` reference. Those were rollout controls, not a requirement of Stripe. The owner explicitly requested ordinary form references, then any email and an audit of test-only behavior across the project.

Both optional filter values are now blank in the deployed Stripe configuration and its source generator. Blank means no recipient or prefix restriction. The existing metadata fields remain, avoiding destructive schema changes. The coordinator requires the actual Primary Contact email, and its saved-request fingerprint uses that email while preserving the shape of earlier fixed-recipient fingerprints. Source identity, pricing, retries, account matching, sandbox/test mode and manual payment confirmation are retained.

No form, email template, workflow timing, price, tax rate, business permission or existing record was changed by this release. The altered production code is one Stripe service; three test classes and one metadata record were deployed, with the generator updated locally. Two general partner-email tests now explicitly use bank transfer: their previous Stripe fixtures depended on exclusion by the former test filter. Dedicated Stripe tests and the two fresh real journeys cover the newly enabled route.
