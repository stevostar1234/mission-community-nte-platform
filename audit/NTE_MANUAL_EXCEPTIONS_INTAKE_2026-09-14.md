# NTE manual-exception audit — intake, pricing and preparation

Internal review, 14 September 2026. This is a source audit of the current working package, with two isolated DOM probes. It does not authorise changes or establish that an actual booking was affected. No Salesforce records, messages, payments, deployments or public pages were changed. All retained UI/INVOICE fixtures and earlier audit histories were left intact.

The owner's new rule permits staff to agree a revised booking price after issuing a Stripe link, reconcile the difference manually and send confirmation by recording Booking payment received. The main task is implementing the saved-Stripe-amount confirmation exception. This review does **not** rediscover that acknowledged veto. It examines whether staff can enter a coherent revised price and whether subsequent preparation work preserves it. The rule and remaining UI decision are now recorded in [desired workflow, line 73](../NTE_DESIRED_WORKFLOW.md#L73).

## 1. Actionable findings

### MEX-I01 — A normal Amount-only price edit can record payment but fail the confirmation

**Priority: high for the newly requested manual workflow. Status: source-proven; final payment implementation is being changed concurrently by the main task.**

The record exposes standard `Opportunity.Amount` as a normal editable field, while also exposing separate editable item charges and `NTE_Listed_Price_Total__c`. There is no maintained Opportunity-update trigger or flow that synchronises those values. The only Opportunity trigger is the after-insert conversion guard; all four maintained record-triggered flows start on Lead. The net total is stored Currency, not a sum formula. VAT and total including VAT are formulas calculated from that stored net and the saved VAT rate. They do not update standard Amount.

**Minimal scenario:** start with exhibition space £800 net, booking net £800, VAT 20%, Amount £960 and Pricing Status `Calculated`. Staff negotiate £840 gross and edit only standard Amount to £840. The panel displays £840 and its receipt preview can record £840 because it checks pricing status/net readiness, not item arithmetic. The confirmation validator then sees £840 ≠ £960, derived from the unchanged £800 net, and rejects the confirmation. Payment remains recorded under the intentional “failed email must not undo payment” rule. Conversely, editing only the net total to £700 updates the formula gross to £840 while the panel and Confirmed Value still prefer the old positive Amount of £960. Editing only the space price leaves both totals unchanged and fails the component-sum check.

**Current workable path:** in that simple example, staff must edit `NTE_Exhibitor_Space_Price__c=700`, `NTE_Listed_Price_Total__c=700` and `Amount=840`, preserving `NTE_VAT_Rate__c=20` and a valid `NTE_Pricing_Status__c`. Formula VAT/gross then update themselves. For a partner, the corresponding main item is `NTE_Sponsor_Package_Total__c`. Power changes must also keep `NTE_Power_Socket_Count__c`, `NTE_Power_Socket_Unit_Price__c` and `NTE_Power_Socket_Total__c` coherent. Initial additional-staff changes use `NTE_Additional_Staff_Count__c`, `NTE_Additional_Staff_Unit_Price__c` and `NTE_Additional_Staff_Total__c`. Setting a formerly unresolved price also requires explicitly resolving `NTE_Pricing_Status__c`; changing Amount alone cannot do that.

**Simplest safe proposal, not implemented:** an “Adjust booking price” action presents existing net components and applicable unit prices, previews VAT/gross, and atomically saves the component sum into net and gross into Amount. It should use saved agreed figures, not reapply today's catalogue or alter issued Stripe/payment history. Make Amount read-only on the NTE record page so it cannot suggest a complete price-edit path. If the owner instead selects arbitrary overall discount/total entry, show an explicit adjustment line and its tax treatment; silently inventing an item allocation would make the itemisation untrue. This decision is separate from accepting a difference from an earlier Stripe request. Preserve the arithmetic validator.

### MEX-I02 — The staff correction form quotes the catalogue price for an existing repriced top-up

**Priority: medium. Status: local DOM result plus existing native preservation-test source; no evidence of money being overwritten.**

**Minimal scenario:** an accepted one-person top-up is manually changed from £50 net to £40 net. Later the applicant corrects its attendee name. The form instructs them to choose Yes and enter the same quantity; doing so displays £50 + £10 VAT, total £60. The server correctly retains the saved £40 net/£48 gross purchase. The form therefore gives a price inconsistent with the retained agreement during a legitimate correction.

- [Staff page:39–46](../exhibitor-staff-update.html#L39) combines purchase and name-correction instructions. [forms.js:529–541](../assets/forms.js#L529) always assigns £50 per person and displays `count * 50`, without booking-state retrieval.
- [Update service:327–344](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L327) only assigns catalogue price on the first positive purchase; an existing positive count preserves its saved price. [Applied acknowledgement values:257–265](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L257) copy that retained price into the receipt Lead.
- Native regression source [saved £60/£120:868–869](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionServiceTest.cls#L868), [browser-price injection:936–942](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionServiceTest.cls#L936) and [retention assertions:952–966](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionServiceTest.cls#L952) explicitly prove the intended preservation contract.

**Local probe:** loading the maintained page and JS in the existing inert DOM harness, selecting Yes/count 1 and correcting a name produced `£50.00 + £10.00 VAT (20%) / Total £60.00 including VAT`, with transport values unit=50, net=50. All assertions passed. The fixture's submit function writes an in-memory array; it performs no HTTP request or Salesforce submission. The saved-price half of the scenario is supported by service/test source, not an invented live booking.

**Proposal:** make the new-purchase tariff and existing-purchase name correction visibly distinct, with a correction route that does not present a newly payable catalogue total. Preserve server ownership of saved prices and the one-time purchase rule. An endpoint that retrieves live prices is not required merely to stop a correction from quoting a new charge.

## 3. Preservation boundaries verified in source

- **No automatic base repricing on preparation.** [Partner staff:320–324](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L320), [exhibitor staff:327–354](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L327), [vehicles:467–488](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L467) and [logo:491–495](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls#L491) update only their owned fields and shared update audit. They do not rewrite base components, net or Amount. Saved accepted positive top-up prices, VAT and payment/document milestones survive ordinary roster corrections.
- **A no-new-top-up correction preserves the existing purchase.** Native source [1519–1581](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionServiceTest.cls#L1519) retains one £60 top-up, its names, finance facts and Primary Contact while updating base names. This is not a cancellation route.
- **Vehicle submissions are complete replacements of logistics, not partial patches.** Omitted optional vehicle/haulier fields are cleared by explicit assignments, so a later complete form can overwrite a prior staff logistics correction. That behaviour is confirmed policy. [Regression:1585–1653](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEUpdateSubmissionServiceTest.cls#L1585) preserves the entire previous snapshot on invalid partial items and clears items 2/3 on a valid complete replacement. Staff should supply the current complete details for the next replacement; do not restore stale hidden values.
- **New conversions price the new booking only.** Native source [433–490](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEConversionFlowTest.cls#L433) checks new current selections while retaining older booking Amount/net/payment facts. Manual Lead derived-price edits are not a supported agreed-price override; conversion prices current application selections.
- **The explicit pricing backfill would overwrite manual base prices, but it is not an automatic release or preparation step.** [NTEPricingService:149–160 and196–225](../force-app/main/default/classes/NTEPricingService.cls#L149) applies current catalogue components, net, VAT and Amount. The [maintenance script:10–15](../scripts/post-deploy/backfill-nte-pricing.apex#L10) selects every NTE application-source Opportunity. [Deployment guidance:73](../SALESFORCE_DEPLOYMENT.md#L73) already requires separate authorisation, an inventory and financial reconciliation and expressly prohibits routine historical repricing. Keep that protection. Its partial-save result handling is the previously qualified P14 limitation, not a newly reproduced ordinary update failure.
