# Form restrictions review — 15 September 2026

Internal handover for the project owner. This reviews the ten maintained submission forms, shared JavaScript, pricing and supplementary reconciliation rules. It distinguishes application choices from technical validation and from restrictions that only become visible after Salesforce receives a submission. It is a source review with the focused release checks below, not a repeat of the completed September end-to-end audit or a guarantee against future platform/configuration failures.

## Approved change

Every organisation category can request every listed exhibitor space. Removed the five category-to-space gates: COBSEO charity, non-COBSEO charity, Local Government Authority, Blue Light and Trade Association. Both browser selection and Lead/Opportunity pricing now accept these combinations. Changing category keeps the selected space. The team still decides whether to approve or reject; submitting or converting an application does not automatically confirm its space.

Both free choices show “Single exhibition space, one 6ft trestle table, one chair and two staff places - worth £499 + VAT.” This is the value of the included bundle, not a charge or an additional discount on paid extras. The two free-option descriptions no longer promise a power discount to every applicant; the power section states its separate existing rule.

No fields or catalogue entries needed deletion. Removed the unused browser eligibility functions/export, two disabled-option CSS rules, and the server eligibility map/helper. Updated the tests that intentionally enforced the old policy. Kept the organisation category field for classification, reporting and power pricing. No saved application or booking was repriced or repaired as part of this release.

## Remaining business constraints to consider

These remain unchanged. A recommendation is not approval to implement it.

| ID | Where | Current behaviour and possible legitimate exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Exhibitor power | Extra sockets cost £100 + VAT, discounted to £50 + VAT only for the two charity categories, Government/Government Agency, Local Government and Blue Light/NHS. A commercial organisation requesting a free charity-labelled space still pays £100 + VAT per extra socket. This is a pricing rule, not a space-selection block. The team can agree a different overall booking total manually. Decide separately whether applicants should request this discount too. |
| R02 | Exhibitor application | Exactly one of the 27 spaces per application; no mixed spaces, several separate singles, custom size or price-on-request choice. A listed double remains one selection. Additional/custom arrangements need separate applications or discussion with the team. |
| R03 | Partner/sponsor application | At least one of 14 priced packages; several different packages are allowed, but no quantity selector for multiple copies, custom-price package or complimentary package choice. Manual total changes, including a full waiver, remain possible in Salesforce. |
| R04 | Applications and partner staff update | The declared attendee total is an integer from 1 to 99. Zero or 100+ cannot be entered. This is independent of the number of names supplied: the system does not count or reconcile names. |
| R05 | Exhibitor power | A positive power answer needs an integer from 1 to 20 extra sockets. A larger requirement needs the team. Garage choices with included power still allow additional paid sockets. Partners report a power need but have no paid socket-quantity control on their application. |
| R06 | Paid finance, both applications | Only Bank transfer or Stripe. There is no cheque/cash/Other choice or split/deposit/instalment request. Staff can discuss exceptions and manually record agreed payment. |
| R07 | Paid finance, both applications | Selecting “Yes” for a purchase order/reference requires its details immediately. Someone who needs a quotation before their PO can be issued has no explicit “reference to follow” choice. This is a useful candidate for relaxation; currently explanatory text can be entered in the details field because it is free text, not a number-only field. |
| R08 | Paid finance, both applications | Legal billing name, billing address and finance contact name/email/phone are required even when invoice and quotation answers are both No, including Stripe. This may be unnecessary for some applicants. Trading name is optional. The team’s ability to use the organisation name on a manually entered booking does not make the form’s legal-name question optional. Proposal 24 confirms the finance contact may be the same person as the main contact, including identical name/email/phone; a separate person is only a preference. |
| R09 | Finance questions | Invoice, quotation, PO/reference and supplier-agreement choices are Yes/No, with no Unsure. Additional-information Yes requires text. Both payment methods expose the same requirements. The entire section is hidden and omitted when an exhibitor total is genuinely £0; adding a paid extra restores it. Partner finance always appears. |
| R10 | Application contacts | Exhibitors must provide job title, email, mobile **and** landline/work phone. They may repeat a contact number, but cannot leave the work-phone field empty. Both application types require a job title. A partner who chooses a different event-day contact must supply that contact’s name, role, email and mobile. The exhibitor second contact is optional. |
| R11 | Other application questions | Exhibitors must answer equipment, heavy-item and accessibility questions, industry/trade membership and discovery source; equipment/membership accept explanatory text such as None. Partners also require the “Anything else to share about your space?” answer. Accessibility Yes requires details. These can feel excessive when nothing further applies. |
| R12 | Sunday setup | The partner answer is required; the exhibitor answer is optional. Both permit No, so inability to set up on Sunday does **not** block an application. The team must review the answer against the selected space. Large-item Yes/Not known yet likewise does not block the application. |
| R13 | Both expressions of interest | Organisation type is a closed list, less inclusive than the exhibitor application’s categories: no dedicated CIC/not-for-profit or student/individual type. “Employer - Other sector” exists but is not an accurate catch-all for every organisation. Organisation, job title, phone, preferred contact method and discovery source are required. |
| R14 | Partner/sponsor interest | Requires at least one interest area, a partner/sponsor/both choice, and additional comments. An applicant cannot leave the comment empty simply because their selections are sufficient. |
| R15 | Exhibitor staff top-up | One positive top-up purchase per booking, 1–99 places, Stripe only, no repeat finance questions. After acceptance, same-quantity name corrections are allowed; self-service increases, reductions, cancellation or refund are not. The owner explicitly retained this rule on 14 September. It is checked against the existing booking after submission, not through a live lookup in the form. |
| R16 | All staff forms | Names must be nonblank and fit the text field. There is no name-count rule, line-per-person rule or automatic headcount inference. The exhibitor top-up needs a nonblank top-up list; combined stored names cannot exceed 32,768 characters. The form cannot include the existing saved top-up list in its local length check when only base names are being corrected. |
| R17 | Logistics | At most three item slots. Item 1 is compulsory. Adding any information for item 2/3 requires its description, dimensions and gross weight, and item 3 requires item 2 first. No fourth item or “details to follow” state. Registrations are optional. |
| R18 | Logistics contacts/haulier | Main and second contact names/email/phone, delivery window and basic haulier details are compulsory. A one-person/self-delivery exhibitor cannot leave them empty; haulier fields explicitly permit N/A. We should decide whether a second contact should be optional. |
| R19 | Logistics delivery times | Only “Sunday 28 February between 14:00 - 18:00” or “Monday 1 March between 06:30 - 07:15”. No alternative date/time choice. These are static NTE27 labels requiring review for a later edition. |
| R20 | Logistics updates | A valid new form replaces the existing logistics details, clearing omitted optional items. It is not a partial edit. The form explains this; the team can correct individual fields manually instead. |
| R21 | Guest registration | One accompanying guest slot, with their first name, surname and email required when Yes. No multi-companion list or option for a companion without email. Main guest phone and organisation are required; the form explicitly permits “Individual” for organisation. Job title is optional. |
| R22 | Volunteer application — Mission Motorsport, not NTE | Requires DOB, mobile/email, address/postcode, emergency contact, at least one opportunity/skill/region, one monthly-hours choice, availability, service/DBS answers, conduct acknowledgement and declaration. “Any” availability is exclusive of named preferences. Emergency contact address is required if different; otherwise the applicant’s address is copied. The closed hours list cannot capture an arbitrary figure; preferences need further discussion. |
| R23 | Volunteer conduct/consent | The conduct reader must be opened and scrolled to the end before acknowledgement. Consent must be answered; the source offers Yes/No, so a No answer is recorded rather than automatically rejected. DBS/service answers are also recorded, not eligibility gates. The new-form conduct acknowledgement/version is additionally enforced in Salesforce. |
| R24 | Logo confirmation | Requires organisation, submitter name/email and booking reference, but no signature/date/tickbox. It records confirmation, not the actual logo file. The separate upload destination is currently unset; this remains a client dependency and the confirmation form cannot verify whether a file exists. |

Highest-value decisions to revisit: PO details that are not available yet (R07), billing details for Stripe without invoice (R08), organisation choices on interests (R13), and logistics capacity/contact/time limits (R17–R19). These are more likely to affect ordinary applicants than malformed-input checks.

## Technical checks retained across the forms

| ID | Check | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 | Required answers | Empty/whitespace-only mandatory text and unanswered required radio/select/checkbox groups show an error before sending. Conditional controls are disabled and omitted while hidden, and become required only when applicable. |
| T02 | Salesforce text capacities | Browser maxlength and submit checks use the mapped Salesforce field limits. Common limits: organisation 255, first name 40, surname/email 80, phone 40, job title 128; long text limits depend on the mapped field. No truncation to silently fit an overlong input. |
| T03 | Email and optional website syntax | Email must be a complete address; no domain allowlist. Optional website controls use native URL validation, so a full URL such as https://example.com is needed when supplied. Phone fields do not impose a UK-only or numeric-only pattern. Names are not restricted to ASCII. |
| T04 | Calendar dates | Declaration dates must be between 1 January 2026 and today in Europe/London. Volunteer DOB is between 1 January 1900 and today. No additional minimum-age test was found in the maintained form. These are real calendar/format checks, not deadline or sold-out checks. |
| T05 | Booking reference | Update forms require an NTE- reference with letters, numbers and hyphens within the supported length. Surrounding whitespace and letter case are normalised. The browser checks format, not whether that booking exists. |
| T06 | Current catalogue | The browser checks selected catalogue values/prices; Salesforce independently calculates amounts and validates known categories, space/package choices, integer quantities and required pricing inputs. Removed/unknown prices cannot become a free booking accidentally. A missing/invalid category can still require pricing review; choosing any actual category never restricts the space. |
| T07 | Duplicate click/bot field | Repeat submit clicks are disabled while sending. A hidden honeypot filled by a bot or unusually aggressive autofill prevents sending and shows a retry error. Navigation back restores the controls. No captcha, login requirement, organisation allowlist, ticket-capacity limit or opening/closing-date gate was found in the maintained forms. |
| T08 | Configuration/JavaScript/transport | The engine needs JavaScript, the configured endpoint/org/return URL/custom-field mappings, and a terms link where used. Missing configuration shows an unavailable error. Review mode shows a local success without sending; current published configuration is sandbox mode. Native form transport cannot guarantee downstream Salesforce acceptance from the generic thank-you redirect. |
| T09 | Event identity | New NTE applications/interests/guests receive a generated event code; callers may supply only a valid NTEYYYY override. A Lead validation rule requires that format. Volunteer intake remains separate. This is not an organisation eligibility rule. |

## Checks that happen after an update reaches Salesforce

The supplementary service requires exactly one accessible Opportunity for the booking reference and the correct staff form for its booking type. Unknown/ambiguous references, wrong staff forms, unsupported form types, missing declarations (except logos), missing roster/logistics information, the existing-top-up restrictions and storage/DML failures can leave a saved update unapplied. A valid but mistyped reference for a different booking is a separate risk: the reference identifies the booking; submitter email/company are not an ownership challenge.

An update may also save successfully but fail to send its acknowledgement because of missing booking contact/email, email delivery limits or a provider/configuration failure. The two states are distinguished in **Update issues**. It is not a universal submission monitor: a failure before Lead creation, a browser/network failure, normal application intake failure or external logo-upload failure cannot be assumed to appear there. Correct a failed update manually or ask for a fresh correct submission, as already agreed; the old issue is not automatically resolved by a new submission.

Native matching/duplicate rules remain unchanged at the owner’s request. Other org automation, access, platform capacity and production-specific configuration can also reject or delay a submission. The earlier end-to-end audit and failure guide document these boundaries; this task does not change shared Salesforce duplicate rules or re-certify the production org.
