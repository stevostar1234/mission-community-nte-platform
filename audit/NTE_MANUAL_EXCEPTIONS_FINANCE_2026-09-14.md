## Findings

### MEX-F01 — A historical Stripe request blocks the selected bank-transfer route (P2, confirmed)

**Scenario:** a first Stripe attempt creates a saved Booking payment request but its email fails. Staff change Payment Method to Bank transfer and use Requirements → “Requirements provided, await payment”.

**Actual outcome:** the action initially accepts BANK, but the worker selects every booking with a saved Stripe request, rejects the now-bank payment method, and marks the booking Failed and the old request Needs review. No bank-transfer email is sent. Retrying cannot resolve the conflict while the request is retained.

**Evidence:** `NTEFinanceService.cls:81–84`; `NTEStripeBookingService.cls:61,73–81,121–123`. Two further dependencies matter to a correction: `emailReadinessError` at `256–266` bypasses Stripe only when no saved request exists, and the provisional renderer at `NTEExhibitorApprovalEmailService.cls:329–344` still uses any saved Booking request for its finance summary/payment button. Merely skipping Stripe preparation would leave another blocker or risk including the superseded link.

**Policy:** the existing manual bank-transfer route already permits changed-charge reconciliation (`NTE_DESIRED_WORKFLOW.md:133` and the 11 September handover's changed-price guidance). Historical requests should remain history without forcing a BANK email through Stripe. This needs a bounded routing fix, not permission to reprice or delete old links. Existing `NTEManualFinanceTest.bankActionDoesNotTickRequirements` (`65–70`) tests a bank booking without a prior Stripe request; it does not cover this transition.

### MEX-F02 — “Retry booking email” targets the wrong email after payment (P2, confirmed recovery UX defect)

**Scenario:** staff record payment and the paid-confirmation dispatch fails. They use the Opportunity's visible “Retry booking email” action.

**Actual outcome:** every Opportunity ID routes to the provisional-email retry. If the earlier provisional is Sent, the action rejects it as already sent. If it previously failed, the action can report Email queued, but the worker rejects the now-paid booking; the failed paid confirmation is never retried. The working recovery is Retry Email on its `BOOKING_CONFIRMED` dispatch in NTE Email Failures.

**Evidence:** record-page action `NTE_Management_Opportunity_Record_Page.flexipage-meta.xml:31`; quick-action label `Opportunity.Retry_Booking_Email.quickAction-meta.xml:3`; `lwc/nteRetryEmail/nteRetryEmail.js:20–27`; `NTEExhibitorApprovalEmailService.cls:89–90,103–109,246–247`. The correct dispatch retry is `NTEEmailDispatchService.cls:235–239,267–331`.

**Policy:** preserve the already-paid guard against another provisional/payment-request email; P01 paid-provisional retries remains unselected. Correct the action's meaning/routing or make the paid-confirmation recovery explicit. `NTEBookingPaymentEmailTest.sendFailureKeepsPaymentAndRetryUsesTheSameDispatchOnlyOnce` proves dispatch-level recovery; `tests/panel-ui-stress.test.js:454–477` covers retry toasts/concurrency but not selecting the correct email after payment.

### MEX-F03 — Notification preparation failure can leave no retryable record (P2, conditional source-confirmed gap)

**Scenario:** `queueBookingConfirmation` throws before its dispatch insert—for example, a temporary user-mode permission/read/insert failure. Payment marking itself succeeds.

**Actual outcome:** the controller catches the notification exception and preserves the payment, as required, but there is no Failed dispatch to retry. After the underlying problem is repaired, the paid button is unavailable and the Opportunity retry follows the provisional route described above. A developer can call the non-Aura queue method; the ordinary operator has no equivalent repair action.

**Evidence:** `NTE_MasterPanelController.cls:875–881,1222`; `NTEEmailDispatchService.cls:204–218`; `lwc/nteRetryEmail/nteRetryEmail.js:20–27`. This is an inferred error-path outcome, not an observed MMUAT failure. Both shipped NTE permission sets currently grant dispatch creation, so no current permission misconfiguration is alleged. Existing missing-contact/template/send-failure tests create a dispatch successfully and do not cover failure before that row exists.

**Policy:** retaining recorded payment is correct. An explicit recovery action that creates a missing confirmation or retries a Failed one while preserving Sent deduplication would satisfy the existing notification/recovery intent; do not solve this by clearing payment or deleting communication history.

## Recovery paths and boundaries already supported

- **Failed paid confirmation after changed contact/amount:** the queued stale snapshot is held, then explicit `retryFailed` refreshes the current Contact and fingerprint (`NTEEmailDispatchService.cls:301–311,407–410`). Contact recovery is covered by `NTEBookingPaymentEmailTest.changedPrimaryNeverReceivesAnOldQueuedSnapshot`; changed-current-amount failure is covered by `changedBookedAmountLeavesThePaymentConfirmationUnsent`. The latter stops at failure, so a subsequent explicit retry after coherent reconciliation is a useful additional case. No old journey needs replaying.
- **Failed provisional followed by real payment:** the receipt service allows it and preserves the earlier failure history; `NTEBookingPaymentEmailTest.failedProvisionalDoesNotDelayRecordedPaymentConfirmation` and `reviewedBaseReceiptAllowsReconciledTransferAfterFailedProvisional` cover this. UI actions remain limited to the requested refiners; this is not grounds to add server sent/checklist gates.
- **Closed/reopened bookings:** the supplied NTE sales process has Closed Lost as its loss state. Active manual actions and queued payment requests reject that state; reopening retains original receipt/request/dispatch facts. Past Close Date and Closed Won are intentionally allowed (`NTEStripeBookingServiceTest.pastCloseDateAndClosedWonDoNotExpireABooking:380–389`). No code closes/reactivates Stripe links or refunds money when staff change stage. That remains explicit manual reconciliation, not a newly found defect. A close-before-worker → reopen → explicit retry test is absent.
- **One later top-up:** a second purchase/reduction through the supplementary form is explicitly refused (`NTEUpdateSubmissionService.cls:418–422`); names can be corrected at the accepted quantity and saved prices are preserved (`327–354`). That confirmed limit should not be widened into an order ledger without a decision.
- **Account/mode, amount/VAT, resource IDs, currency, URL, inactive-link and duplicate-request checks remain useful.** None needs weakening to send a manually reconciled paid confirmation. Root's renderer fix should remain independent of Stripe transport and preserve the historical request.
